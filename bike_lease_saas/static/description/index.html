Frequently Asked Questions

How does bike leasing and contract management work?
Customers submit lease applications specifying bike models, rental duration, and identity documents. Upon approval, the system checks vehicle availability, allocates a bike, and automatically generates an official Lease Contract.
How are payments, installments, and security deposits handled?
Security deposit invoices are generated to activate contracts upon payment. Itemized installment schedules calculate recurring lease charges, daily vehicle surcharges, and late fees with single-click invoicing.
How are bike returns, maintenance, and inspections managed?
The vehicle return wizard records final odometer readings, battery levels, and inspection condition to issue deposit refund or penalty invoices. Servicing issues automatically create maintenance requests in Odoo Fleet.
