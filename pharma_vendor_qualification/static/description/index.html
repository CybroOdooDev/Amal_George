Pharmaceutical ERP — Vendor Qualification

Vendor qualification, Approved Vendor List (AVL) and purchase-order AVL enforcement for the Pharmaceutical ERP suite.

Odoo 19 Live Benchmarks Role-Based Security

Supply Chain Compliance

Qualify vendors through a public portal questionnaire, maintain an Approved Vendor List, and enforce QA approvals on purchase orders.

The Core Pillars

Why Pharmaceutical ERP?

Approved Vendor List (AVL)

Maintain a centralized repository of approved vendors and their compliance statuses.

Public Portal Questionnaire

Vendors can complete qualification questionnaires securely via a token-based web portal.

Purchase Order Enforcement

Enforce QA-approval workflows and automatic AVL verification on purchase order confirmation.

Process Filtering

Filter valid vendors seamlessly on POs, BOM components, and incoming receipt forms.
Settings

Activate the Feature

Turn on vendor qualification from Pharmaceutical ERP > Configuration > Settings. This unlocks the qualification menus and enforces vendor approval checks on purchase orders.

Purchase

Access from the Purchase App

Once enabled, a Vendor Qualification menu appears under Purchase, giving QA and purchasing teams a central place to manage qualifications, questionnaires, and the Approved Vendor List.

Questionnaire

Build a Questionnaire Template

Define reusable question sets (e.g. GMP compliance, documentation practices) that QA can send to any vendor to evaluate their quality systems before approval.

Qualification

Start a Vendor Qualification

Create a qualification record for the vendor and the products they'll supply, then send them the questionnaire. This generates a secure portal link and moves the record to Questionnaire Sent.

Portal

Vendor Fills Out the Portal Form

The vendor opens the emailed link and answers the questionnaire through a public portal, no login required, then submits it back for review.

Audit

Schedule a Follow-Up Audit

After the vendor submits their answers, QA can schedule an on-site or documentation audit to verify the responses before scoring.

Audit Approve

Score and Approve the Vendor

QA reviews the questionnaire answers and audit results, assigns a score, and approves the vendor, adding them to the Approved Vendor List for the qualified products.

Purchase Order

Purchase Order Approval

With the vendor approved, a purchase order can be raised and submitted for manager approval. Confirmation is blocked until that approval is granted, enforcing the QA-approved vendor list at the PO stage.

QC Specification

Define QC Specifications

Before or after goods receipt, QA sets the acceptance criteria (test parameters, min/max limits) each incoming product must meet. This spec is what incoming test results get checked against.

Quarantine Queue

Received Goods Held in Quarantine

Incoming lots are automatically placed in quarantine on receipt, blocking their use until tested. QC staff launch a test order directly from this queue.

QC Test

Record Test Results

QC enters actual test values against the specification's limits for each parameter. The system flags out-of-spec results, and the order is signed off as Passed or Failed, releasing or rejecting the lot accordingly.

AVL Management

Track and control which vendors are approved to supply specific materials.
Self-Service Portal

Eliminate emails with direct portal access for vendors to submit qualification details.
PO Validation

Prevent purchase of materials from unapproved or expired vendors automatically.
Smart Filtering

Only show valid, approved vendors when selecting suppliers for BOMs or POs.
How do vendors submit their details?
Vendors receive a secure, token-based link to a public portal where they can complete the qualification questionnaire.
What happens if a vendor is not approved?
The system will prevent purchase orders from being confirmed if the vendor is not on the Approved Vendor List for that product.
Can this module be disabled later?
Yes, uninstalling returns the Purchase Order and BOM processes to their native behaviour without AVL enforcement.
