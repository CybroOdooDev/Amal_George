Shift Working Times Overview (Kanban View)

Navigate to Shifts → Configuration → Shifts to view all department-specific shift working times organized as intuitive Kanban cards with department tags.

Shift Schedule & Department Configuration

Configure shift parameters including the associated Department, rotational Sequence number, and weekly working hour intervals across different periods.

Accessing the Schedule Generation Action

Quickly initiate mass employee shift generation directly from the top navigation bar by selecting Shifts → Shift → Generate Schedule.

Mass Shift Schedule Generation Wizard

Specify the target Department, Start Date, and End Date in the generation wizard. Click Generate to automatically schedule and rotate shifts for employees.

Department Employees Selection & Verification

Verify all employees under the selected department who are subject to the automated shift rotation.

Assigned Shift Schedules on Employee Profile

Each employee's profile displays the assigned shifts under the dedicated Shift Schedule tab, complete with Start Date, End Date, and assigned Shift calendar, protected by overlap validation.

Dynamic Payslip Worked Days & Payroll Computation

Upon payslip creation and computation, worked days and attendance/leave hours are dynamically calculated based on the employee's active shift schedule for that pay period, ensuring 100% payroll precision.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Enterprise Open HRMS Employee Shift module?

This module enables HR teams to create, manage, and track employee shift schedules within Odoo 19 Enterprise. It supports multiple shift types, contract-linked assignments, and integrates shift data directly into payslip computation.

Can I assign different shifts to different employees?

Yes. Each employee can have their own shift schedule configured directly on their profile or linked through their employment contract, allowing full flexibility for diverse workforce arrangements.

Does this module integrate with Odoo Payroll?

Yes. The module integrates with Odoo's HR Payroll (hr_payroll), allowing shift-based hours and schedules to be reflected accurately in payslip calculations.
