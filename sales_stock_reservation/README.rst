Product Stock Reservation
=========================
Allow sales users to create a product stock reservation directly from the sales quotation form.

Key Features
------------
* **Direct & Partial Stock Reservation:** Reserve products directly from draft quotations with customizable quantities per line via an interactive wizard.
* **Instant Inventory Protection:** Automatically generates confirmed stock moves to immediately reduce forecasted availability and prevent overselling.
* **Automated Email Notifications:** Sends reservation confirmation emails to customers and selected internal team members.
* **Reserved Stock Tracking & Chatter Audit:** Track reserved quantities in a dedicated tab on the quotation and automatically log details in the chatter.
* **Flexible Cancellation & Auto-Release:** Release reserved stock on demand or automatically when the quotation is confirmed into a sale order.

Installation
------------
No external dependencies.

Configuration
-------------
No additional configuration is required.
