How does the Pro IMEI Lookup API work?

When you enter a 15-digit IMEI, Odoo connects to ImeiCheck.com's endpoints (using your key and optionally PHP Service IDs). It automatically retrieves the brand, model, and manufacturer, creating Odoo brand/model catalog records on the fly and populating your form.

Does it prevent installing incompatible spare parts?

Yes! The Pro module overrides the parts selector line, filtering the available items list based on the target Brand and Model configurations of the device. Technicians are restricted to compatible parts, minimizing repair mistakes.

What reports are generated, and in what format?

The Pro module includes Complaint Type, Mobile Parts, and Mobile Service reports. All three reports can be generated as traditional PDF summaries or as fully formatted Excel (XLSX) spreadsheets that stream directly to your downloads.
