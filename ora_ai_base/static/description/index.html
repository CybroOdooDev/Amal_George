Answers Before You Install

Q
What external services are required to use Ora-Ai Base?
A
Ora-Ai Base connects to Vapi.ai for real-time voice orchestration. You will need a Vapi account with Public and Private API keys, configured easily under Voice Assistant Settings. Vapi handles the orchestration between your chosen AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, Groq), transcriber (Deepgram, Gladia, Speechmatics), and ElevenLabs for natural voice synthesis.
 
Q
How does the voice assistant create sales orders and invoices?
A
The assistant leverages custom Vapi function-calling tools that trigger secure webhooks in Odoo. During conversation, the assistant verifies customer identity, collects quantities, verifies variant IDs, and calls the webhook to generate real records in sale.order or account.move. You can choose whether voice sales orders remain as draft quotations or are automatically confirmed.
 
Q
Can the assistant answer questions from our company documents?
A
Yes. Through the Voice Assistant > Files menu, you can upload proprietary documentation (PDF, DOCX, CSV, TXT, Markdown up to 25MB). These documents automatically sync with Vapi's canonical storage for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). The assistant strictly confines its knowledge to your company's domain, grounding responses directly in your uploaded documents.
 
Q
Does Ora-Ai Base support Odoo 20 Community and Enterprise?
A
Yes, it is fully compatible and tested for Odoo 20 Community Edition and also operates on Odoo 20 Enterprise, Odoo.sh, and On-Premise deployments. It only depends on standard modules: sale_management, mail, and account.
