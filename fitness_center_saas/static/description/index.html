Enrollment Workflow ⭐

Follow the complete operational flow of member subscriptions—from creation, invoice automation, and active session generation, to expiry and renewals.

Draft Invoice Active Sessions Expired Renew
1

Step 1 — Create Enrollment

Create a new enrollment by selecting the member, membership plan, trainer, fitness class, and personal training package. The system automatically generates the enrollment number (ENR/00021) and calculates the membership end date.

2

Step 2 — Generate Invoice

Generate the membership invoice directly from the enrollment. The invoice is automatically linked to the enrollment and contains the applicable membership and service charges.

3

Step 3 — Payment & Activation

Once the invoice is paid, the enrollment becomes Active and the system automatically initiates training session generation. Relevant notifications are also sent to the member and assigned trainer.

4

Step 4 — Automated Training Sessions

Active enrollments automatically generate training sessions incrementally. Sessions are generated up to the current month's end and expanded automatically through scheduled background processing, while duplicate sessions are prevented.

5

Step 5 — Expiration & Renewal

When an enrollment reaches its end date, it automatically moves to Expired. Staff can renew the membership to continue the subscription or cancel it to stop future session generation.

The Fitness Center SaaS module uses an incremental session-generation engine to efficiently schedule member training sessions. When an enrollment becomes active, sessions are generated only through the end of the current month. A daily scheduled process then expands the schedule into upcoming months while respecting the enrollment end date and preventing duplicate sessions.
